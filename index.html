Performance report pages

Each page here belongs to one examination paper. It opens when the QR code at the top of a candidate’s performance report is scanned, and draws that candidate’s report again from the code: the same PDF they were given.

To see a report, scan the QR code on it.

Nothing about any candidate is stored here. A candidate’s details travel inside the QR code’s link, after the #, which a browser does not send to this site.
